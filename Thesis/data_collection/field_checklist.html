1. Before leaving

  • Drone batteries charged (enough for all zones + spare)
  • Raspberry Pi in its case, both cameras fixed, blue filter on the NoIR lens
  • Pi power (drone battery regulator or power bank, 5 V / 5 A)
  • Pi set to capture at power-on — no ~/socfa_no_capture file
  • Laptop charged; server.js and app.py start without errors
  • Phone hotspot on and charged (Pi, laptop and soil nodes connect to it)
  • Soil nodes charged / powered
  • Grey or white reference card
  • Leaf Color Chart (LCC)
  • Reference instruments: soil thermometer; pH meter and EC meter (if available)
  • Soil sample bags, marker, small trowel (on lab-sample days)
  • Flags / stakes for zone corners (if not already in the field)
  • This checklist, pens, clipboard; phone for ground photos

2. Setup at the field

  1. Check zone flags are in place (Zone A, B, C…). Note any that moved.
  2. Turn on the hotspot; connect the laptop.
  3. Start server.js and app.py. Open the Soil page: every node Online, no Check probe.
  4. Fix any probe that is loose or not in the soil before continuing.
  5. Place the grey/white card flat on the ground inside the first zone.

3. For each zone

a) Drone

  1. Install the 3 nodes (see the schedule) and wait 30 min; the Soil page shows all 3 Online, readings steady.
  2. Power on the drone + Pi; wait until the NDVI page finds the Pi. Note the time: ______
  3. On the NDVI page, type the spot (e.g. A-01) and pick Pairs (3 is enough).
  4. Fly to the + of that spot and hover at 7 m. Press Take Photos; keep hovering until the status says the photos are done.
  5. Fly to the next spot (the Spot box has already gone up by one: A-01 → A-02) and repeat until A-06.
  6. Land. Open From Drone and check each spot's Used pair shows the right place. Re-fly any bad spot with the same spot label.
  7. Write down the spot (and the flight name if you can, e.g. flight_20261102_101530).
  8. Safe shutdown before removing the battery (press the Pi's power button once; wait for the steady red light).

b) Crop rating (ground truth) — rate before looking at the system's result

  1. Walk each spot (A-01 … A-06). Rate it with the rubric below: Healthy / Moderate / Poor; one row per spot on the log sheet.
  2. LCC: compare ~10 leaves (youngest fully expanded leaf, shade the leaf with your body). Write the average.
  3. Take 3–5 phone photos: overview, close-up of leaves, any pest/disease/damage.
  4. Note water level (cm of standing water, or saturated/dry) and anything unusual.

c) Soil reference (objective 2)

  1. At each node: reference thermometer (and pH/EC meter if available) right next to the probe, same depth. Record the sensor value shown on the Soil page at the same time.
  2. Lab days: take a soil sample at the probe, bag it, label Zone–Node–Date, record the sensor values at that moment.

d) System results

  1. Screenshot the Field Assessment page for the zone; write the soil class, final class and score on the log sheet before moving the nodes.

Crop rating rubric (palay)

ClassWhat you see in the zoneLCC (guide)
HealthyUniform dark-green canopy, even height and stand, no visible stress; pests/disease absent or very minor.Mostly ≥ 4
ModerateLight green or patchy yellowing, uneven growth or gaps, some wilting or leaf damage on a small part of the zone.Around 3
PoorWidespread yellowing/browning, stunted or sparse plants, dry cracked soil, or damage on a large part of the zone.≤ 2

Agree the rubric with your adviser or an agriculturist before the first field day, and keep it the same for the whole study. LCC values follow the chart used (check its instructions for your variety and planting method). Use the same observer for all ratings if possible.

Same conditions every time: 10:00–14:00, clear or steady light, same flight height, same zones. Don't fly in rain, strong wind, or partly cloudy light that changes during the flight.

4. End of the day

  • All zones flown; every spot of today's 3 zones (A–C or D–F, -01 … -06) rated and written down; running total updated
  • Log sheets filled in; copy them into validation_log.csv and sensor_reference_log.csv the same day
  • Back up the captures folder and phone photos (e.g. Google Drive)
  • Soil samples labelled and stored (cool, sealed) for the lab
  • Pi shut down safely; batteries charging for next time
  • Do not delete real flights
